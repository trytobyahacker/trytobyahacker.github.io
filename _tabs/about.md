## whoami

I'm **MiLordd** — security researcher and bug bounty hunter based in Spain.

I've been in cybersecurity since 2024, and in under a year I've gone from zero to hunting bugs on real targets, earning monetary bounties, reporting critical vulnerabilities to companies generating +$750M/year, receiving **3 Letters of Recognition from NASA**, and winning multiple hacking competitions.

---

## The beginning

After finishing school I was lost, I didn't know what direction to take. I heard my aunt mention that computer engineering pays well, so I enrolled in a vocational IT course. Since school I'd always been the tech guy: helping classmates with Word, terminal tips, getting Kahoot answers with GitHub tools. But the course wasn't for me, the teachers explained things poorly and I felt stuck.

Then I watched **Mr. Robot**.

That show changed everything. I asked myself: *"Why not become a hacker?"* There were no hackers where I was from, I'd never heard anyone talk about hacking in my country. So I thought: why not be the first?

I asked a teacher how to get started in cybersecurity. He told me to learn JavaScript. But I didn't have the resources, not even a computer, so I couldn't start yet.

That summer I worked moving furniture and picking fruit with my uncle. At some point he looked at me and asked:

> *"Do you really want to work like this your whole life? Carrying heavy things, suffering, working for others?"*

That hit me hard. I thought about it for a long time.

---

## The grind

In 2024 I finally saved enough to buy a computer and started learning on my own, YouTube, HackTheBox, TryHackMe, PortSwigger, public bug reports, making contacts in the community.

When I had around **53 HTB machines** completed I didn't want to stagnate. So I started testing real local businesses, walking into shops in person and reporting bugs directly to the managers. That's how I became a freelancer, combining security work with building websites for small businesses that had none, powered by AI tools.

From there I moved to bug bounty platforms, focusing heavily on **Web and APIs**. Now I'm expanding into **Mobile app security**.
